Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. GeoChronicle respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through compliance with this policy.

Information Collection and Use

We collect and use your location data to provide historical walking tours and landmark information relevant to your surroundings. This data is processed on-device and is not shared with third-party servers.

Data Persistence

Your saved landmarks and activity data are stored locally on your device using the Room database. We do not transmit this data to our servers.

Third-Party APIs

We use the MediaWiki API to fetch historical landmark information. Please refer to their privacy policies for information on how they handle data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
